About

I've designed software since 2020: first at Deloitte Digital across client projects, then at Amazon on an open-source evidence archive for police agencies, and since 2023 at Intuit, on AI tools for its tax and bookkeeping experts.

Most of my work answers one question: when should software act on its own, and when should a person decide? At Intuit that meant a confidence threshold for renaming customer documents. At ContxtIQ, a startup I co-founded, it means grading interview answers live while the recruiter stays in charge of the call.

I prototype in Figma and in code. At ContxtIQ I ship interface changes to the production app myself.
